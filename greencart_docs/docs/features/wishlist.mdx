# Wishlist

Save products you like and buy them later.

---

## Features

- **Heart icon** on every product card throughout the app
- **Wishlist tab** in bottom navigation
- **Move to Cart** — add item to cart and remove from wishlist
- **Remove** — swipe or long-press to remove
- **Empty state** — illustration when wishlist is empty
- **Badge count** — shows number of saved items

## How to Add to Wishlist

Tap the **heart icon** on any product card:

- Home screen product strips
- Category product list
- Product details page
- AI recommendation cards

The heart fills with red when the item is saved.

---

## Moving to Cart

On the Wishlist screen:
1. Tap **Move to Cart** on any item
2. Item is added to cart
3. Item is removed from wishlist
