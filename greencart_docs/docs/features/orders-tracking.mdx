# Orders and Tracking

View your order history and track deliveries in real-time.

---

## My Orders

A list of all past and current orders.

### Each Order Shows
- Order number and date
- Product thumbnails (up to 3)
- Item count and total amount
- Status badge with color

### Status Colors

| Status | Color |
|--------|-------|
| Pending | Yellow |
| Processing | Blue |
| Shipped | Orange |
| Delivered | Green |
| Cancelled | Red |

Tap any order to track its progress.

## Track Order

Step-by-step order tracking with animated timeline.

### Tracking Steps

```
✓ Order Placed        — Mon, 10 Jun 2026 09:15 AM
✓ Order Confirmed     — Mon, 10 Jun 2026 09:20 AM
✓ Packed              — Mon, 10 Jun 2026 11:00 AM
● Out for Delivery    — Mon, 10 Jun 2026 02:30 PM
○ Delivered           — Expected: Mon, 10 Jun 2026 05:00 PM
```

### Each Step Shows
- **Checkmark** — completed step
- **Spinner** — current step
- **Circle** — pending step
- **Timestamp** — when completed
- **Connecting line** — solid (done) or dashed (pending)
